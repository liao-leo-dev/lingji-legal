Goblinocracy is a single-player card roguelike about winning goblin elections by any means necessary. This page answers common questions and tells you how to reach us.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Runs, statistics, and achievements are stored locally. There is no cloud sync and no account, so deleting the App erases your progress — we cannot restore it.

How do I switch the language?

From the main menu tap Settings, then under Language choose 中文 or English. The change applies immediately and is remembered the next time you open the game.

How do I request a refund?

App Store purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report a bug?

Email us and include:

  • your device model (e.g. iPhone 15),
  • your iOS version,
  • the game version (shown on the App Store page),
  • what you were doing when it happened — which screen, which cards or candidates were in play, and what you expected instead.

Contact us

Need help or have a suggestion? Email lingjihelp+goblinocracy_support@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days.
