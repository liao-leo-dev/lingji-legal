Privacy Policy — Goblinocracy

Effective date: 2026-10-05

This policy applies only to the mobile game Goblinocracy.

Language: English | 繁體中文

This Privacy Policy explains how LingJi Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Goblinocracy (the "App"). It applies only to Goblinocracy.

Information we collect

None. The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in of any kind.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • The App works entirely offline and makes no network requests of its own.

Game progress and achievements

Your run progress, statistics, and achievements are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the App deletes this data with it; there is no cloud sync and we have no copy to restore.

Children

We do not collect personal data from anyone, of any age — including children. The App contains satirical election gameplay and simulated in-game currency only; there is no real-money wagering.

App Store purchases

If you buy the App or any content through the App Store, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's handling of that data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Effective date" will change accordingly.
